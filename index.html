Why LocalTools?

Private by design

Every tool runs on your device. Smallpdf, iLovePDF and most online tools upload your files to their servers — LocalTools has nothing to upload to.

No free-use rationing

Other sites let you do a few tasks a day, then ask you to pay or wait. Here there is no counter, no account, no paywall — use a tool 100 times a day if you like.

Works offline

Once a page has loaded, you can disconnect from the internet and the tool still works. Handy on planes, in the countryside, or on flaky Wi-Fi.

No sign-up, no tracking

No account, no email, no ads following you around. Nothing about you is collected, because there is nothing to collect it with.

Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes — all of them, unlimited use, no account and no email. They run in your browser, so there is no server cost per task to pass on to you.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. That is the whole point of LocalTools. Your PDFs, images and text are processed by JavaScript in your own browser and never leave your device. You can test it: load a page, turn off Wi-Fi, and the tool still works.

Why not just use Smallpdf or iLovePDF?

Those are good tools, but they upload your files to their servers and limit free use to a few tasks a day. If your file is private — a contract, a medical form, a family photo — LocalTools keeps it on your device.

Which browsers work?

Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari. The PDF merger and image tools use standard browser features from the last few years.

Is there really no daily limit?

Really. There is no account, so there is nothing to count against. Merge 50 PDFs, compress 200 photos — the only limit is your device.
