    Frequently asked questions

    How much smaller will my images get?

    A typical phone photo (3–5 MB) drops to 200–500 KB at 80% quality — roughly 90% smaller — with no visible difference on screen.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Compression runs in your browser via canvas. Your images never leave your device.

    What is WebP?

    A modern image format that is 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same quality. All current browsers support it.

    Can I compress PNGs with transparency?

    Yes — choose PNG output to keep transparency. JPG and WebP (lossy) do not preserve it the same way; JPG fills transparent areas with white.

    What quality setting should I use?

    80% is the sweet spot for photos on the web — visibly identical to the original at a fraction of the size. Use 90%+ for images people will zoom into, 60–70% for thumbnails.

    Does compressing work on screenshots?

    Yes. Screenshots compress very well as PNG output (lossless, keeps text crisp) or as JPG at 85–90% if you don't need transparency.
