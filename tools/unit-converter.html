Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the conversions?

Exact for defined units (1 inch is exactly 25.4 mm). Results are shown to 10 significant figures.

Does it work offline?

Yes — after the page loads, all conversion is plain arithmetic in your browser.

Which units are included?

Length (mm to miles), weight (mg to tonnes), volume (mL to gallons), area (cm² to acres), temperature (C/F/K), speed (m/s, km/h, mph, knots) and data (bytes to TB).

Why is 1 US gallon different from 1 imperial gallon?

This converter uses the US gallon (3.785 L), the one used in the United States. The imperial gallon (4.546 L) is used in the UK.

Why are there two kinds of gallon?

This converter uses the US gallon (3.785 L), the one used in the United States. The imperial gallon (4.546 L) is used in the UK — if you need it, convert litres instead.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h). It is the standard speed unit for ships and aircraft.
