Password Generator — Strong random passwords, made locally

Free password generator that runs entirely in your browser. Create strong random passwords with your own length and character rules — nothing is sent anywhere.

Privacy first: this tool runs 100% in your browser. Your files and text are never uploaded to any server — there is no server here to upload to. You can even turn off your Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works. No sign-up, no account, no tracking.

How it works

Three steps, no account, no waiting room.

  1. Choose a length — 16 characters is a good default; longer is stronger.
  2. Pick character sets — mix letters, digits and symbols.
  3. Copy and use it — store it in a password manager; never reuse it.

Good for

  • A unique password for every account
  • A strong Wi-Fi password
  • A temporary password for a shared account
  • A master password for your password manager

Frequently asked questions

Are the passwords really random?

Yes. They are built with your browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues), the same source used for security keys.

Do you store or see my passwords?

No. Generation happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent, saved or logged anywhere.

How long should a password be?

At least 16 characters for important accounts. Each extra character multiplies the guessing time — a 16-character mixed password has about 100 bits of entropy.

Why do some sites reject the password?

Some sites limit which symbols are allowed. Uncheck symbols, or remove the ones the site rejects, and generate again.

Should I use the same password on two sites?

No — never. If one site leaks its passwords, every account sharing that password is at risk. Generate a fresh one per site and keep them in a password manager.

Is 16 characters enough?

For most accounts, yes: a 16-character password mixing letters, digits and symbols has around 100 bits of entropy, which is far beyond what anyone can brute-force. Go longer for your email and password manager.
