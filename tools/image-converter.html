    Frequently asked questions

    Which format should I choose?

    JPG for photos, PNG when you need transparency or crisp text, WebP when you want the smallest file on a modern website.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Conversion runs in your browser. Your images never leave your device.

    Does converting lose quality?

    PNG is lossless. JPG and WebP are lossy — quality 90% is visually near-identical to the original for most photos.

    Can I convert many images at once?

    Yes — select as many as you like; each one converts and downloads separately.

    Will converting a JPG to PNG make it better quality?

    No — converting can't add detail that was lost when the JPG was made. It just changes the container. PNG output is useful when you need transparency or lossless storage.

    Why does my PNG turn white when I convert to JPG?

    JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. If you need transparency, convert to PNG or WebP instead.
