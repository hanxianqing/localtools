PDF Merge — Combine PDF files into one, free

Free PDF merger that runs entirely in your browser. Combine multiple PDFs into one file — your files never leave your device, no sign-up, no limits.

    Privacy first: this tool runs 100% in your browser. Your files and text are never uploaded to any server — there is no server here to upload to. You can even turn off your Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works. No sign-up, no account, no tracking.

    How it works

    Three steps, no account, no waiting room.

    1. Choose your PDFs — pick two or more files. They are read on your device only.
    2. Reorder them — use the ↑ and ↓ buttons, or remove files you don't need.
    3. Merge and download — one combined PDF downloads to your computer.

    Good for

    • Joining chapters of a report into one file
    • Combining scanned pages into a single document
    • Merging invoices or receipts for an expense claim
    • Assembling a portfolio from separate PDFs

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a file size or page limit?

    No. The merge happens in your browser, so the only limit is your device's memory. Files with dozens or hundreds of pages work fine.

    Do my PDFs get uploaded anywhere?

    No. LocalTools has no server that accepts uploads. Your files are read by your browser and never leave your device.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    No — encrypted PDFs cannot be opened in the browser. Remove the password first, then merge.

    Does the merged PDF keep the original quality?

    Yes. Pages are copied as-is, so text, images and formatting are unchanged.

    Can I merge more than two PDFs?

    Yes — add as many as you like. There is no file-count limit; the only practical limit is your device's memory.

    Does the order of the files matter?

    Yes, and you control it: use the up and down arrows next to each file to arrange the pages in the order you want before merging.
